## Architecture Overview

The **Fleetmikewood Protocol** is an informational and biophysical framework for real-time epigenetic regulation and phenotypic optimization. 

> **Important Clarification:** This protocol does not involve physical nucleotide sequence modification (DNA editing/CRISPR). It operates entirely within the parameters of transcriptional regulation, real-time biological feedback loops, and epigenetic modification (such as DNA methylation and histone modifications).

### Core Operational Principles

1. **Epigenetic Regulation over DNA Editing:** Treats the human genome as a dynamic read/write system where chemical tags dictate gene expression, rather than altering fixed nucleotide sequences.
2. **Informational Engineering:** Leverages AI-designed linguistic, cognitive, and somatic scripts during high-neuroplasticity states (specifically the nightly hypnagogic window) to systematically downregulate HPA-axis stress responses and optimize autonomic recovery.
3. **Healthspan Target:** Focuses on phenotypic optimization and stress-response resilience targeting a functional baseline healthspan of 120+ years.
